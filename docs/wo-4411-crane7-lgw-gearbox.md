## Summary

Hoist gearbox on crane 7 showing rising vibration on daily walk-round checks
over roughly three weeks before failure -- the driver reported the hoist
feeling "rougher" on wet lifts before the sensor numbers moved. Vibration
climbed steadily, then spiked sharply in the final 48 hours; bearing
temperature followed the same curve about a day behind. Gearbox seized
mid-lift on 2025-11-03. No cargo drop, no injuries.

## Findings

Stripped down: primary bearing race spalled, consistent with slow-onset
fatigue rather than a single shock load.

## Corrective action

Replaced hoist gearbox assembly and primary bearing set. Re-instrumented the
vibration sensor mount -- the previous mount had drifted out of position,
which may have delayed detection of the early trend.

## Parts used

Hoist gearbox assembly; primary bearing set; vibration sensor mount.

Labour: 22 hours.
