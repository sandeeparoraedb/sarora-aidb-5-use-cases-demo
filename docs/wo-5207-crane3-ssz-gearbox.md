## Summary

Crane 3 hoist gearbox failure -- same signature as WO-4411: vibration
trending up over the prior 2-3 weeks, sharp acceleration in the last 2-3
days before failure, temperature drift lagging vibration by roughly a day.
Failed mid-vessel-call, forcing a crane swap and a 3-hour berth delay.

## Findings

Post-mortem: bearing race fatigue, likely accelerated by a run of
back-to-back heavy lifts in the weeks prior.

## Corrective action

Replaced hoist gearbox assembly and primary bearing set.

## Recommendation

Flag any crane showing more than 2x baseline vibration sustained over 48+
hours for inspection -- don't wait for a hard fault code to trigger the
work order.

## Parts used

Hoist gearbox assembly; primary bearing set.

Labour: 16 hours.
